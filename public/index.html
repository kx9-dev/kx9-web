Questions

Before you deploy.

Can the AI move more money than I allow?

No. The Shield contract checks every trade against your limits before it's sent. The AI core has no way to change those limits. Only your signature can.

Do I need to approve every trade?

In Supervised mode, yes. In Autonomous mode the unit trades by itself, but only inside your Shield limits. Standby stops everything at once.

What can a unit trade?

Assets on Robinhood Chain that you add to its whitelist, such as stock tokens and crypto. Availability of stock tokens may depend on your country.

Is KX-9 affiliated with Robinhood?

No. KX-9 is an independent project built on Robinhood Chain, a public blockchain. It is not made, endorsed or operated by Robinhood Markets.

Will my unit make money?

There's no guarantee. Units follow rules, but markets move and AI can make mistakes. Start small and treat this as a tool, not a promise.
