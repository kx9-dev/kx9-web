Privacy Policy

What data KX-9 collects, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have.

Effective 28 October 2026 · Version 1.0

1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy explains how [KX-9 operating entity legal name] ("KX-9", "we") handles personal data when you use the KX-9 website, command app, documentation and API (the "Services"). We act as the data controller for the data described here.

We never ask for, collect or store your private keys or seed phrase. We don't collect government ID through the Services.

3. Public blockchain data

Robinhood Chain is a public blockchain. Your wallet address, Shield vault, ERC-8004 identity, Shield settings and every on-chain transaction are permanently visible to anyone and cannot be deleted by us or by you. The Flagship Unit's Black Box is public by design. Black Boxes of other units are visible to their operators through the app, and the underlying on-chain events are public.

We don't sell personal data and we don't use it for third-party advertising.

5. AI processing

To form proposals and write reasons and mission reports, the Core sends your unit's market context, holdings, protocol settings and Shield limits to an AI model provider acting as our processor. We send the minimum needed, don't include your wallet address where it isn't required, and contractually prohibit the provider from using this data to train its models.

6. Who we share data with

  • Infrastructure providers: hosting, databases, RPC and node providers, monitoring.
  • AI model providers: as described above.
  • Blockchain networks: anything you submit on-chain becomes public.
  • Authorities: when required by law or to protect rights and safety.
  • Successors: if the project is reorganised or transferred, under this policy.

A current list of processors is available on request at the contact below.

7. How long we keep data

  • Account and unit configuration: while your unit is active, then 24 months.
  • Off-chain Black Box mirrors and simulations: 24 months, unless you ask for earlier deletion.
  • Technical logs: 30 days; security logs up to 12 months.
  • Support messages: 24 months after the conversation ends.

On-chain data cannot be deleted.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under the EU/UK GDPR and Indonesia's Personal Data Protection Law (Law No. 27 of 2022), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing of your data, to data portability, and to withdraw consent. To use these rights, contact [privacy contact email]. We'll respond within the time the law requires. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

9. International transfers

Our providers may process data in countries other than yours. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

10. Security

We use encryption in transit, access controls, least-privilege keys and monitoring. No system is perfectly secure; please protect your wallet and devices.

11. Cookies and local storage

The Services use only what's needed to work: session storage for sign-in and local storage for display preferences. We don't use advertising cookies. If we add analytics, we will update this policy and ask for consent where required.

12. Children

The Services are not for anyone under 18. We don't knowingly collect data from children.

13. Changes to this policy

We'll post updates here with a new effective date and notify you in the app of material changes.

Current Command app data

KX-9 stores wallet addresses, five-minute sign-in challenge hashes, hashed session tokens with a 24-hour expiry, saved protocol settings, verified unit state and decision records in PostgreSQL. Private keys and RPC credentials are not included in these records or returned to the browser. Historical-test CSV files are parsed in the browser; submitted numeric price observations are processed for the response and are not retained. Signed-in users can export or explicitly delete off-chain account data in Protocols. Public blockchain records cannot be deleted by KX-9. Deleting off-chain data does not move funds or revoke on-chain permissions.
