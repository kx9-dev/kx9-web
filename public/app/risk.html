Risk Disclosure

The main risks of using autonomous AI trading units, stock tokens and $KX9. Please read this before funding a unit.

Effective 28 October 2026 · Version 1.0

Draft for legal reviewThis document is a working draft prepared for the KX-9 project. Text in [brackets] must be completed, and the whole document should be reviewed by qualified counsel in the operating entity's jurisdiction before launch.

1. General

Using KX-9 involves significant risk, including the possible loss of everything you place in your Shield vault. Only use funds you can afford to lose. This disclosure doesn't list every risk.

2. Market risk

Prices of crypto assets and stock tokens can move sharply and quickly. Protocols like Guard can't guarantee exits at your chosen levels, especially in fast or illiquid markets. Past results, simulations and Flagship Unit performance do not predict future returns.

3. AI and automation risk

AI agents can make mistakes, misread data, act on stale information or behave unexpectedly. In Autonomous mode trades happen without your approval each time. Shield limits cap how much a unit can trade, not whether a trade is a good one.

4. Smart contract risk

Shield vaults, ERC-8004 registries, token contracts and the exchanges your unit trades on are software and may contain bugs or vulnerabilities, even after audits. Exploits can lead to permanent loss.

5. Blockchain and network risk

Robinhood Chain is a Layer 2 network. At launch it relies on a single sequencer operated by Robinhood; if it stops, transactions, including trades and Standby, may be delayed. Congestion, reorganisations, RPC outages and bridge issues can also affect your unit.

6. Stock token risk

Stock tokens track listed shares but are not the shares themselves. They may not carry voting or other shareholder rights, may trade at a premium or discount, may be paused or redeemed by their issuer, and may be unavailable or restricted in your jurisdiction.

7. Liquidity and slippage

Thin markets can cause large price impact. Your slippage limit may block trades you wanted, or a trade may fill at the worst price your limit allows.

8. Data and oracle risk

Units rely on price feeds and market data from third parties. Wrong or delayed data can lead to wrong proposals and wrong USD valuations for Shield checks.

9. $KX9 token risk

$KX9 may have little or no liquidity and its price may be highly volatile or fall to zero. It gives access to features only and no claim on profits or assets. Feature thresholds can change.

10. Regulatory risk

Laws on crypto assets, tokenised securities and AI agents are changing. New rules could restrict the Services, specific assets or $KX9 in your country, or require changes to how KX-9 works.

11. Key and operational risk

If you lose your wallet keys, no one can recover your Shield vault. Phishing sites and fake contract addresses are common; only use addresses listed in the Docs.

12. Tax

Each trade may be a taxable event. You're responsible for your own tax reporting.

13. Acknowledgement

By using KX-9 you confirm you have read and understood these risks and accept them. If you're unsure, seek independent professional advice before using the Services.

Current integration limits

RPC connectivity is not proof of trading readiness. No default vault, price history, trade fill, identity attestation or reputation score is manufactured.
